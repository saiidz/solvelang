## Commercial truth rules

Do not claim:

- existing customers unless documented,
- proven conversion rates,
- guaranteed savings,
- established market pricing for SolveLang,
- production SaaS execution that does not exist,
- compliance certifications or SLAs that do not exist.

Pricing in this packet is a hypothesis to test. ROI calculations require customer-provided inputs. Case studies should only be published with permission and should separate measured results from estimates.

## Initial offer strategy

Start with fixed-scope services:

1. workflow clarity audit,
2. workflow prototype sprint,
3. automation rescue/documentation,
4. implementation in the client's chosen stack,
5. optional monthly maintenance.

SolveLang can be used as the readable specification, review, and handoff layer even when another platform executes the workflow.
